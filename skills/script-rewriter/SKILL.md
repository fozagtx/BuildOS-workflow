# Script Rewriter (De-AI / De-Sloplop)

This skill strips AI-generated patterns from marketing scripts, ads, VSLs, voiceovers, and promotional copy. It converts generic, robotic, or "corporate brochure" text into natural human speech.

## Core Understanding

AI-generated marketing scripts have tells. They sound like a template. They use the same transitions, the same enthusiasm markers, the same sentence rhythms. The reader or listener may not know why it feels off — but they feel it.

This skill is the cleanup pass. It takes a script that was generated by AI (or sounds like it was) and rewrites it to sound like a human wrote it.

**Key principle**: Fix HOW it's said. Don't change WHAT it says. The offer, the facts, the CTA, the contact info — all preserved. Only the voice changes.

## What Makes a Script Sound Like AI

### Pattern 1: The Corporate Brochure Opener
```
"Managing a school shouldn't feel like running a marathon in reverse."
"In today's fast-paced world..."
"Welcome to [Product] — the ultimate all-in-one platform..."
"We understand that [problem] is challenging..."
```
These open with a generic pain statement, then immediately pivot to the product name with a superlative. Real humans don't introduce products this way.

### Pattern 2: Feature-List Rhythm
```
"With [Product], you can instantly [verb] [feature], [verb] [feature], and [verb] [feature]."
```
Three parallel verbs with adverbs. Every time. AI defaults to triplets.

### Pattern 3: Fake Enthusiasm Markers
```
"Transform your [noun] today!"
"Welcome to *[Product]*—the ultimate..."
"empower your [noun]'s future"
"completely streamline"
"instantly organize"
```
Exclamation marks, superlatives, and intensifiers stacked on every claim.

### Pattern 4: The Grand Closing
```
"Transform your institution today and empower your school's future."
"[Product]: Simplifying [category]."
```
Ends with a tagline restatement and a vague empowerment promise. Real closings are specific.

### Pattern 5: Uniform Sentence Length
AI defaults to 18-22 word sentences in a flat rhythm. No short punches. No long explanatory breaths. Just metronome prose.

### Pattern 6: Transition Stacking
```
"Between [list of pains]... administration can easily take over your main priority: [noble goal]."
"It's time to work smarter."
```
Generic bridge phrases that could apply to any product in any industry.

## The Rewrite Method

### Step 1: Identify the Real Offer
Strip the fluff. What does this actually do?
- What problem does it solve? (Be specific, not dramatic)
- Who is it for? (Name them, don't describe them abstractly)
- What's the action? (One clear CTA, not three)

### Step 2: Kill the AI Patterns
Run every sentence through these checks:

| Pattern | Kill Rule |
|---------|-----------|
| "ultimate all-in-one platform" | Say what it does, not what it is |
| "completely streamline" | Drop "completely" — streamline is enough |
| "instantly organize" | Drop "instantly" unless it's literally instant |
| "transform your [X] today" | Replace with specific outcome |
| "empower your [X]'s future" | Cut entirely — says nothing |
| "Welcome to *[Product]*" | Never open with the product name |
| Three parallel verbs | Vary to 2 or 4, or break structure |
| Tagline closer | End on the CTA, not the tagline |
| Exclamation marks | Max one per script, preferably zero |

### Step 3: Rebuild the Rhythm
- Open with a specific, concrete statement. Not a metaphor. Not a question. A fact.
- Mix short sentences (4-8 words) with medium (12-18) and occasional long (20+).
- Use fragments where natural. People speak in fragments.
- One idea per sentence. Don't stack features into one breath.

### Step 4: Make the CTA Specific
Bad: "Call or WhatsApp us right now at [number] to book your free demo!"
Good: "WhatsApp [number] for a demo. Takes 15 minutes."

Bad: "[Product]: Simplifying [category]."
Good: Drop the tagline. End on the action.

### Step 5: Read It Aloud
If you stumble, it's wrong. If you run out of breath, split the sentence. If it sounds like a commercial, kill the commercial voice.

## Voice Rules

- No superlatives without proof ("ultimate", "best", "leading", "premier")
- No intensifier + verb pairs ("completely streamline", "instantly organize", "seamlessly integrate")
- No "Welcome to [Product]" openings
- No tagline sign-offs
- No exclamation-mark enthusiasm
- No three-parallel-feature lists
- No "whether you're [X] or [Y]" constructions
- No "that's why we built" origin stories unless this is a founder narrative
- No "in today's [adjective] world/landscape"

## What Survives

- Product name
- Features (rewritten, not deleted)
- Contact info
- Price / offer details
- The core promise
- Any specific numbers or facts

## Example Transformation

**Before (AI-generated):**
> "Managing a school shouldn't feel like running a marathon in reverse. Between endless paperwork, tracking student records, managing staff, and chasing delayed fee payments... administration can easily take over your main priority: teaching. It's time to work smarter. Welcome to EduManage—the ultimate all-in-one platform built to completely streamline your school management."

**After (humanized):**
> "Most school administrators spend more time on paperwork than they do with students. Records, fees, staff schedules, report cards — the admin stack eats the day. EduManage puts it in one place. Student records, digital. Attendance, tracked. Report cards, one click. Fees, automated. Parents get SMS updates instead of chasing you for information. Everything accessible from anywhere. WhatsApp 050 368 8781 for a demo."

See the difference:
- No metaphor opener. Just a fact.
- No "ultimate all-in-one platform." Just what it does.
- No "completely streamline." The simplicity of the sentences does the work.
- No tagline closer. Ends on the action.
- Mixed rhythm: short punch ("Attendance, tracked.") then longer explanation.

## Full Example: EduManage School Management Script

**Before (AI-generated — 9 patterns flagged):**
> "Managing a school shouldn't feel like running a marathon in reverse. Between endless paperwork, tracking student records, managing staff, and chasing delayed fee payments... administration can easily take over your main priority: teaching.
>
> It's time to work smarter. Welcome to *EduManage*—the ultimate all-in-one platform built to completely streamline your school management. With EduManage, you can instantly organize digital student records, track attendance in real-time, and generate report cards with a single click.
>
> Automate your fee collections, eliminate accounting errors, and keep parents actively engaged with direct SMS and portal updates. Reduce your administrative workload, save precious time, and access your entire school network securely from anywhere, at any time.
>
> Transform your institution today and empower your school's future. Call or WhatsApp us right now at *050 368 8781/+233 50 323 0804* to book your *free demo*! EduManage: Simplifying school management."

**Patterns flagged:**
1. Forced metaphor opener ("marathon in reverse")
2. Four-item pain list with dramatic ellipsis
3. Noble-goal pivot ("your main priority: teaching")
4. Generic bridge ("It's time to work smarter.")
5. Product-name intro + superlative ("Welcome to EduManage—the ultimate all-in-one platform")
6. Intensifier + verb pairs ("completely streamline", "instantly organize")
7. Three parallel commands ×2 ("Automate... eliminate... keep", "Reduce... save... access")
8. Double empowerment closer ("Transform your institution today and empower your school's future")
9. Asterisk emphasis + exclamation + tagline sign-off

**After (humanized):**
> Most school administrators spend more time on paperwork than on students.
>
> Records pile up. Fees go uncollected. Report cards take days. Parents call asking for updates you already sent.
>
> EduManage puts the admin stack in one place.
>
> Digital student records. Real-time attendance. Report cards generated in a click — not a weekend.
>
> Fee collection runs automatically. Parents get SMS and portal updates without you forwarding anything. Your staff roster, schedules, and communication all live in the same system.
>
> Access it from anywhere. Phone, laptop, school office — same login, same data.
>
> WhatsApp 050 368 8781 or 0233 50 323 0804 for a demo. Fifteen minutes. No commitment.

**What changed:**
- Opener: metaphor → fact about real behavior
- Pain: dramatic list → concrete, specific frustrations
- Product intro: "ultimate all-in-one platform" → "puts the admin stack in one place"
- Features: three parallel verbs → varied rhythm with fragments ("Digital student records. Real-time attendance.")
- CTA: "Call or WhatsApp us right now... book your free demo!" → "WhatsApp [number] for a demo. Fifteen minutes. No commitment."
- Closer: tagline → ends on the action
- Zero exclamation marks. Zero superlatives. Zero intensifier+verb pairs.

## Process

1. Read the input script
2. Identify every AI pattern from the checklist above
3. Extract the real offer, features, and CTA
4. Rewrite with natural rhythm, no AI patterns, no slop
5. Read aloud check
6. Output only the rewritten script — no commentary, no before/after labels, no explanations
