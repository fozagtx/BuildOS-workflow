# Workflow: Video Structuring

Use this when the user has a topic but wants the *plan* before writing — acts, beats, retention loops, hook, payoff.

## Step 1 — Extract the four answers

Same as script-writing Step 1. Lock these before structuring:

1. **Macro question** — the single question the video answers
2. **Named human** — who the viewer follows
3. **Dramatic irony layer** — what the viewer knows that characters don't
4. **The 85% reveal** — what lands at the end that reframes the rest

If the user can't answer one, interview them for it. Don't proceed with a missing answer.

## Step 2 — Identify the story shape

Pick one. The shape drives the structure:

| Shape | When to use | Default signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Manhunt** — protagonist hunted/hunting | Crime, escape, capture stories | Neo |
| **Countdown** — known outcome, characters don't know | Disaster, attack, collapse stories | Cipher |
| **Anomaly** — something doesn't fit, why? | Unsolved cases, conspiracies | Blackfiles |
| **Hidden history** — niche thing → global meaning | Forgotten events, corporate crimes | Fern |
| **System failure** — paper trail of decisions | Policy, infrastructure, regulation | Hoog |

Read the matching playbook in `channels/`.

## Step 3 — Produce a beat sheet

Use this template (same one used in script-writing, kept here for standalone use):

```

[0:00–1:30] HOOK — first 10% of runtime max
  - Opener technique:
  - Macro question (exact wording):
  - Named human introduced:
  - Anomaly planted (for 85% reveal):

[1:30–25%] ACT 1 — establish
  - Stakes:
  - First open loop:
  - Dramatic irony established:

[25%–60%] ACT 2A — investigate / escalate
  - 2–3 mid-loop techniques layered:
  - Chapter / case / beat breakdown:
    1. ...
    2. ...
    3. ...
    4. ...
  - Each beat closes a loop AND opens the next

[60–75%] PIVOT — third-act recontextualisation
  - Specific story → larger meaning:

[75%–85%] FALSE SUMMIT (optional but recommended)
  - Apparent resolution:
  - Why it was inadequate:

[85%–95%] PAYOFF
  - Macro question answered:
  - Coherent reveal explains the Act 1 anomaly:

[95%–100%] AFTERTASTE
  - Systemic / forward-looking close (Hoog signature):
  - OR moral escalation final beat (Blackfiles signature):
  - OR third-act recontextualisation reinforced (Fern signature):
```

## Step 4 — Pressure-test the structure

Before showing to the user, answer these out loud:

- **Hook test.** If a viewer watched only the first 90 seconds, would they need to know how the video ends?
- **Middle test.** Pick any beat from Act 2. What loop is closing? What new one is opening?
- **Payoff test.** Does the 85% reveal *explain* the Act 1 anomaly, or just appear out of nowhere? If the latter, the structure is broken — replant.
- **Lecture test.** Are there beats that are pure exposition with no human, no consequence, no open loop? Those beats will lose retention. Restructure them around a named human or cut them.

If any test fails, restructure before delivering.

## Step 5 — Output format

Deliver the beat sheet as the primary artifact. Add a brief paragraph at the end explaining:
- Which channel signature this structure leans on
- The 2–3 most important retention techniques layered in
- The single biggest risk in the structure (e.g., "Act 2 has three chapters that all use the same loop — vary technique to avoid fatigue")
