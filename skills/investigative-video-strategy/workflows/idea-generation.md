# Workflow: Idea Generation

Use this when the user wants topic ideas that fit these retention patterns. Most documentary ideas die because they don't have the structural ingredients — the goal here is to surface only ideas that *can* be made into a Neo/Cipher/Blackfiles/Fern/Hoog video.

## Step 1 — Get the input

Ask the user for at least one of:
- A **domain** they want to cover (e.g., aviation disasters, corporate fraud, intelligence operations, urban planning failures)
- A **channel signature** they want to emulate (Neo, Cipher, Blackfiles, Fern, Hoog)
- A **constraint** (e.g., must be a story most people haven't heard, must be recent, must have public records)

If they only give a vague request ("ideas for an investigation channel"), pick 1–2 of the above and propose options across them.

## Step 2 — Apply the qualification filter

Every idea you propose must pass ALL of these:

1. **There is a macro question.** A single specific question the entire video would answer. If you can't write it in one sentence, kill the idea.
2. **There is a named human at the centre.** Real or composite. If the topic is purely systemic with no human face, ask whether a composite character can carry it (Hoog approach).
3. **There is at least one anomaly the audience won't see coming.** If the entire story is predictable from the premise, retention dies in Act 2.
4. **There is dramatic irony available.** The viewer can be told something — outcome, betrayal, hidden flaw — that the people in the story don't know. If you can't engineer this, the story will read as a lecture.
5. **There is a third-act payoff that's bigger than the premise.** A way the niche story connects to something universal. Without this, the video stays "interesting" instead of "important."

**Reject ideas that fail any of these.** Don't soften the rule. A good idea is rare; padding the list with bad ones wastes the creator.

## Step 3 — Output format

For each idea, deliver:

```
IDEA #N — [working title, max 60 chars]

CHANNEL FIT: [Neo / Cipher / Blackfiles / Fern / Hoog]

MACRO QUESTION: [one sentence]

NAMED HUMAN: [who the viewer follows — name and role, even if composite]

ACT 1 ANOMALY: [the thing that doesn't fit, planted early]

DRAMATIC IRONY: [what the viewer knows that the characters don't]

85% REVEAL: [the coherent explanation that lands at the end]

THIRD-ACT RECONTEXTUALISATION: [how the specific story becomes universal]

WHY IT WORKS: [1–2 sentences — the structural reason this video can sustain 20+ minutes]
```

## Step 4 — Volume rule

Default to **3 strong ideas**, not 10 weak ones. If the user wants more, generate more — but never lower the qualification bar.

If you can only find 1 idea that passes the filter for the user's domain, say so. Suggest adjacent domains where more ideas are available.

## Step 5 — Source plausibility check

For each idea, briefly note what kind of source material would be needed:
- Public records / court documents
- Investigative journalism that already exists
- Primary interviews
- Open-source intelligence (OSINT)

If an idea requires sources the creator likely can't get, flag it. A great idea you can't research is useless.
