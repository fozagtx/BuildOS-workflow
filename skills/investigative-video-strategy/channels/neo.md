# Neo — Channel Playbook

## Signature move

**Parallel narrative.** A second, completely separate story runs alongside the main one. Same crisis, same failure mode, same stakes. It plays out to its full conclusion before the main story resumes. When the viewer returns, the emotional weight has multiplied without a single new piece of exposition.

Two storylines for the price of one. The secondary story is not a detour — it is a mechanism for making the primary story hit harder.

## Full move-set

### 1. Parallel narrative (signature)
- Pick a mirror story that shares the core failure mode of the main story
- Let it run uninterrupted to full resolution before returning
- The mirror story's payoff conditions the viewer's emotional response to the main story's payoff
- Choose a mirror story the audience hasn't heard — discovery beats recognition

### 2. Investigation chain
- Each piece of information logically leads to the next, like breadcrumbs
- The viewer is never given the answer — they are given the next clue
- Every section closes one question and immediately opens another
- The video does not feel like a documentary. It feels like a detective case the viewer is solving in real time

### 3. Cold open rewind
- Open at the moment of maximum tension — the capture, the crisis, the consequence
- No context, no introduction, no topic setup
- Then immediately rewind to the beginning to explain how that moment was reached
- The viewer already knows something extraordinary happens. They stay to understand how.

## When to use Neo signature

- Manhunt stories (pursuer + pursued)
- Investigations where evidence is uncovered piece by piece
- Stories with a known dramatic moment that can anchor a cold open
- Any topic where a parallel historical/contemporary case exists

## Voice and pacing

- Spare. Declarative. Minimal adjectives.
- Information density is high — every sentence either advances the chain or plants a future loop
- Transitions between parallel and main story are clean cuts, not bridges
- No throat-clearing. No "before we get into it." The cold open IS the cold open.

## Common failure modes

- **Parallel narrative becomes a distraction.** Fix: ensure the mirror story shares the *core failure mode*, not just the surface domain.
- **Investigation chain has gaps.** Fix: every clue must logically produce the next; if you're skipping steps, the viewer feels lectured.
- **Cold open rewind is contaminated with context.** Fix: the first 60 seconds must be pure tension — no setup, no narrator explanation. Trust the viewer to be confused for 60 seconds.

## Channel-specific signature checklist

- [ ] Cold open lands at maximum tension with zero context
- [ ] One mirror story runs to full conclusion alongside the main story
- [ ] Every Act 2 section closes a loop AND opens a new one
- [ ] By the end, both storylines' payoffs are integrated, not parallel
