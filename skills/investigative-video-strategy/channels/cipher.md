# Cipher — Channel Playbook

## Signature move

**Dramatic irony as the entire engine.** The viewer is told critical information before the people inside the story know it themselves. The threat, the countdown, the inevitable failure — all revealed to the audience first. Every scene the characters experience normally, the viewer experiences as a countdown.

No cheap music. No manufactured tension. The audience becomes a co-conspirator in slow motion, watching events unfold with knowledge the characters don't have.

## Full move-set

### 1. Dramatic irony engine (signature)
- Front-load what the viewer needs to know to feel tension on every scene
- Reveal the threat, the betrayal, the systemic flaw — early
- Every subsequent character action becomes loaded
- The viewer is not waiting for surprise; they are waiting for *recognition* — when the characters realise what the viewer already knows

### 2. Self-contained case study structure
- Break long videos into distinct mini-documentaries
- Each case has its own beginning, mechanism reveal, and resolution
- Each case study is a complete story in its own right
- Every case closes with a payoff, then immediately raises stakes for the next
- The viewer is never watching a lecture — they are watching a series of crimes (or incidents), each bigger and more sophisticated than the last

### 3. Human anchor inside the systemic story
- Every large-scale story (hijacking, cyberattack, geopolitical crisis) is filtered through ONE named person at the centre
- Their decisions, psychology, specific situation carry the viewer through dense content
- When the facts become dense, the human story pulls the viewer back
- The character does not need to be sympathetic. They need to be specific enough that the viewer cannot stop watching what happens to them.

## When to use Cipher signature

- Geopolitical events with a known outcome
- Crime stories with a clear failure trajectory
- Intelligence / cyber / military operations
- Any story where the ending is publicly known and the viewer can be primed with it

## Voice and pacing

- Clinical. Procedural. The voiceover sounds like a debrief, not a story.
- Time markers used relentlessly ("six hours before the attack," "two weeks earlier") — they reinforce the countdown
- Multiple case studies stacked, each tighter than the last
- The human anchor is reintroduced by name in every scene they appear in — never "he" or "she" alone

## Common failure modes

- **Dramatic irony is implied, not stated.** Fix: be explicit. Tell the viewer the threat upfront. Trust them to hold it.
- **Case studies blur into each other.** Fix: each case must have a clear mechanism reveal that makes it distinct from the last.
- **Human anchor gets lost when systemic context expands.** Fix: cut back to the anchor at the end of every dense exposition block. Use their reaction as the bridge.

## Channel-specific signature checklist

- [ ] Within the first 2 minutes, the viewer knows what the characters don't
- [ ] Video is structured as 3+ self-contained case studies, each escalating
- [ ] Every case study has a named human at its centre
- [ ] Each case study closes a loop AND escalates the stakes for the next
- [ ] The final case study reframes everything that came before
