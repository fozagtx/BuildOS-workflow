# Monetization and Safety

Use this file when the user asks about YouTube Partner Program readiness, monetization rules, inauthentic content, AI disclosure, reused content, or pre-application checks.

## Important Boundary

Monetization rules change. Before giving current thresholds or policy advice, check official YouTube Help pages. Do not rely only on old course notes, screenshots, or social posts.

## Current Threshold Summary

As of the official YouTube Help information checked on 2026-07-03:

### Expanded YPP Access

Creators in eligible countries may apply for earlier access when they have:

- 500 subscribers
- 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days
- And either 3,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months or 3 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days

### Full Revenue Sharing

For full ad revenue sharing and additional benefits:

- 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months
- Or 1,000 subscribers and 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days

Shorts views from ads, private videos, unlisted videos, deleted videos, and other invalid sources do not count in the same way as valid public Shorts views.

## 2025 Inauthentic Content Clarification

YouTube clarified that repetitive or mass-produced inauthentic content is not eligible for monetization. Treat pure AI spam, identical templates, low-effort slideshows, and copied compilations as high risk.

The goal is not just to upload more. The goal is to make videos that feel original, useful, and intentionally produced.

## Reused-Content Risk

High-risk patterns:

- Downloading viral clips and reposting with minimal edits
- Movie or streamer clips with no meaningful commentary
- Repetitive AI scripts over similar stock footage
- Same template, same voice, same structure, no new value
- Watermarked clips from other creators

Lower-risk patterns:

- Original narration or commentary
- New script and angle
- Licensed or royalty-free visuals
- Meaningful editing and sequencing
- Educational context
- Clear story structure and payoff

## AI Disclosure

YouTube requires disclosure for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content when it could mislead viewers about real events, people, or places.

Check disclosure when using:

- Realistic AI voice in sensitive or identity-related contexts
- AI-generated realistic people
- Altered footage that changes what appears to have happened
- Voice cloning or likeness recreation

Minor edits, color correction, captions, or non-realistic AI visuals may not need disclosure, but official guidance should decide.

## Pre-Application Checklist

Before applying for monetization:

1. Confirm thresholds in YouTube Studio.
2. Check there are no active Community Guidelines strikes.
3. Fill out channel basics.
4. Turn on required account security.
5. Confirm the channel has one AdSense for YouTube path.
6. Review the last 30-60 uploads for:
   - Reused content
   - Watermarks
   - Repetitive formats
   - Misleading titles
   - Unlicensed audio
   - Realistic AI disclosure issues
7. Private or delete risky low-effort uploads.
8. Keep proof of licenses, scripts, voiceover files, and editing process.

## If Rejected

1. Read the rejection reason.
2. Audit the whole channel, not only the latest upload.
3. Remove or improve risky content.
4. Add more original narration, commentary, and structure.
5. Reapply only when the channel clearly looks original and policy-safe.
