# Voiceover and Audio

Use this file when the user needs TTS tools, voice direction, narration settings, or audio cleanup.

## Goal

Make narration sound clear, human, and fast enough for Shorts without becoming robotic or hard to understand.

## TTS Tool Stack

The supplied knowledge base lists these free or generous no-login options. Availability, limits, and commercial terms can change, so verify before production.

1. SPEECHMA: https://speechma.com
2. Luvvoice: https://luvvoice.com
3. Crikk Text to Speech: https://crikk.com/text-to-speech
4. NoteGPT TTS: https://notegpt.io/text-to-speech

- Google AI Studio as a free alternative

## Voice Direction

Match the voice to the niche:

| Niche | Voice Style |
|-------|-------------|
| Commentary | Confident, quick, slightly skeptical |
| Streamer clips | Energetic, reactive, casual |
| Movie recap | Dramatic, suspenseful, clear |
| Storytelling | Warm, mysterious, emotionally paced |

Default direction:

```text
Read this as a fast-paced YouTube Shorts narration.
Keep it clear, curious, and conversational.
Emphasize the hook and the payoff.
Avoid sounding like an announcement.
```

## Process

1. Paste the final script into the TTS tool.
2. Generate 2-3 voice options.
3. Pick the clearest version, not just the most dramatic.
4. Export audio as MP3 or WAV.
5. Listen once while reading the script.
6. Regenerate lines that sound mispronounced, too slow, or too flat.

## Audio Enhancement Workflow

Use Audacity, CapCut, a DAW, or similar tools.

Recommended chain:

1. Normalize
2. Treble boost
3. Low roll-off for speech
4. Light bass boost
5. Bass and treble balance
6. Compressor
7. Limiter

Goal: loud, clean, cinematic speech without distortion.

## Safety Rules

- Do not clone a real person's voice without permission.
- Do not impersonate a public figure or creator.
- If the audio is realistic and AI-generated in a context that could mislead viewers, check YouTube's AI disclosure requirements.
- Keep a copy of the script and TTS source for future monetization review or appeals.
