# Channel Foundation and Warm-Up

Use this file when the user is starting a new YouTube Shorts channel, deciding whether to post immediately, or asking for a Day 0-7 setup plan.

## Goal

Make the channel look like a real creator account with a clear niche signal before publishing. The warm-up phase should create normal usage history, not fake engagement or automation.

## Beginner Warm-Up Plan

### Day 0-1: Create and Wait

1. Create a fresh Google account or use an older account if available.
2. Create the YouTube channel.
3. Set only basic info:
   - Channel name
   - Handle
   - Profile picture
4. Avoid heavy customization at first.
5. Do not post immediately on a brand-new account.
6. Leave the channel untouched for at least 24 hours. If the user is cautious, wait 48 hours.

Notes:

- Phone verification can be done later when the channel needs advanced features.
- Do not use fake identity tricks, automated browsing, or engagement bots.
- Turn on account security features when preparing for monetization or long-term use.

### Days 2-5 or 2-7: Light Activity

Log in daily from the same normal device and network when possible.

Daily activity:

1. Watch 20-40 minutes of videos in the exact niche.
2. Search normally, as a human would.
3. Watch full videos when useful, or at least 50-70 percent.
4. Like a few videos naturally.
5. Leave 2-5 thoughtful comments only when there is something real to say.
6. Subscribe to 10-20 relevant channels across the warm-up period, not all at once.
7. Optionally create one or two playlists.
8. Optionally add videos to Watch Later.

The purpose is niche alignment and normal account behavior. It is not a method for manipulating views.

## Channel Basics

### Name

Use a simple, niche-neutral name that can survive future pivots.

Good patterns:

- Short phrase
- Easy spelling
- No excessive symbols
- No copyrighted brand names
- No narrow joke that blocks future topics

### Profile Picture

The course notes recommend a human face instead of a random logo. If the user wants a faceless brand, use a clean character, object, or bold symbol that still feels human and memorable.

### About Section

For Shorts, banners and long descriptions are less important early. Still, before monetization review, fill the channel out cleanly:

- What the channel covers
- Who it is for
- Contact email if appropriate
- No income claims, impersonation, or misleading promises

## First Upload Readiness Gate

Before the first upload, confirm:

- The channel is at least 24-48 hours old.
- The user has watched relevant niche content for several days.
- The niche is selected.
- The user has at least 5-10 scripts or ideas ready.
- The first videos use original narration or commentary.
- The visuals are licensed, royalty-free, or meaningfully transformed with rights considered.
- No bought views, bought subscribers, or spam comments are planned.
